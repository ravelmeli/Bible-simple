## ➤ Video description : 

"God won't give you more than you can handle": that sentence appears nowhere in the Bible. In one minute, let's look at the real verse it probably comes from, 1 Corinthians 10:13, and what it actually promises.

The text is about temptation (Greek peirasmos), in a context of idolatry, not about every suffering. The promise: God will not let the temptation overwhelm you, and he prepares a way out. Paul himself writes, in 2 Corinthians 1:8, that he was "weighed down exceedingly, beyond our power". So you can be overwhelmed without lacking faith. If you are at the end of your rope, ask for help, from God and from the people around you.

📖 KEY VERSE: "God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted above what you are able, but will with the temptation also make the way of escape." — 1 Corinthians 10:13 (WEB)
