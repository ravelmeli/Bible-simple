"God won't give you more than you can handle." That sentence is not in the Bible.
People say it to comfort someone who is hurting: a loss, an illness.
It probably comes from a real verse, misunderstood: 1 Corinthians 10, verse 13.
In the World English Bible: "God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted above what you are able, but will with the temptation also make the way of escape."
The Greek word is peirasmos: a temptation, a test of faithfulness. Paul is talking about the risk of falling into idolatry, not every suffering in life.
And the promise is not: "God sends you what you can carry." It says: God will not let the temptation overwhelm you, and he prepares a way out.
Paul himself writes, in 2 Corinthians 1, verse 8: "weighed down exceedingly, beyond our power, so that we despaired even of life."
So you can be overwhelmed without lacking faith.
If you are at the end of your rope, ask for help, from God and from the people around you.
