## ➤ Description de la video : 

Did King David really exist? Some scholars doubted it, until a discovery made in 1993 at Tel Dan, in northern Israel. In one minute, discover the stone that mentions the "House of David."

A fragment of black basalt, reused in a later wall, carries an inscription in Aramaic. A king of Damascus, probably Hazael (a hypothesis: his name is not preserved), boasts of killing the king of Israel and the king of the "House of David," meaning David's dynasty. The text dates to the 9th century BC, about 150 years after David's reign. It is the first widely accepted mention of David outside the Bible, and it comes from an enemy. The surprising part: the Bible also tells of the deaths of these two kings, Joram and Ahaziah, but credits them to Jehu (2 Kings 9). This stone doesn't prove the whole biblical story, but to his neighbors, the House of David was real.
