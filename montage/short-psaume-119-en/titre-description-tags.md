## ➤ Video description : 

Psalm 119 is the longest chapter in the Bible: 176 verses. Psalm 117 has only two, and just one psalm sits between them (118). Why so many verses? Because it is a poem built like an alphabet.

In Hebrew, the text is an acrostic: 22 stanzas, like the 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet. Each stanza has 8 verses, all beginning with the same letter, from alef to tav (8 × 22 = 176). Almost every verse speaks of God's word, with words like law, precepts or commandments. It is not a cold code: the author says he loves this word, finds comfort in it, and also begs God in hardship. The text does not name its author.

📖 KEY VERSE: "Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path." — Psalm 119:105 (WEB)
