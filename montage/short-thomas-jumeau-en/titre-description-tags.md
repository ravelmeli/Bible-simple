## ➤ Video description : 

Which apostle had the nickname "the twin"? Thomas. The Gospel of John introduces him as "Thomas, who is called Didymus": "Thomas" comes from Aramaic, "Didymus" from Greek, and both mean "twin". Twin of whom? The Bible does not say.

Ancient non-biblical writings, like the Acts of Thomas, make him the twin of Jesus: it is a late tradition, with no support in the Gospels. He is often called "the doubter" because of John 20 (a traditional nickname, not in the text), but the Gospel also shows a courageous disciple (John 11:16) and the strongest confession of faith in John: "My Lord and my God!" (John 20:28).
