## ➤ Description de la video : 

Who am I really required to love? A teacher of the law asks Jesus: "And who is my neighbor?" In one minute, discover Jesus' answer: a story that turns the question upside down.

On the road from Jerusalem down to Jericho, about 17 miles and more than 3,000 feet of descent, robbers leave a man half dead. A priest passes by and avoids him, then a Levite; the text doesn't say why. Then comes a Samaritan, a man the Jews avoided. Moved with compassion, he treats his wounds with oil and wine, takes him to an inn and pays two denarii, about two days' wages. Jesus then asks which of the three was a neighbor to the wounded man. The teacher answers, "He who showed mercy on him," without even saying "the Samaritan." The question is no longer "who deserves my help?" but "who will I be a neighbor to?"

📖 KEY VERSE: "Now which of these three do you think seemed to be a neighbor to him who fell among the robbers?" He said, "He who showed mercy on him." Then Jesus said to him, "Go and do likewise." — Luke 10:36-37 (WEB)
