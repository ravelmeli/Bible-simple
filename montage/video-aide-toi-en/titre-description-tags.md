## ➤ Video description :

"God helps those who help themselves": many people think it is a Bible verse. It is not. In this study we follow the sentence's trail, from La Fontaine's fable "The Carter Stuck in the Mud" (1668) and its Greek source in Aesop, to the English proverbs of George Herbert, Algernon Sidney and Benjamin Franklin, before looking at what the Bible really says about God's help.

The biblical text speaks of a help that comes from God (Psalms 33, 46 and 121, the Hebrew word ezer), of salvation by grace (Romans 5, Ephesians 2) and, at the same time, of real effort: the ant in Proverbs, 2 Thessalonians 3, the Red Sea (Exodus 14), Nehemiah, Proverbs 21:31 and 16:9, Psalm 127. We also see why the sentence can hurt people who are suffering (Job 42), what the Bible asks for the poor (Leviticus 19, Proverbs 19) and why it does not stand against care: a doctor, a psychologist or someone close to you can help.

📖 KEY VERSE: "My help comes from Yahweh, who made heaven and earth." — Psalm 121:2 (WEB)
