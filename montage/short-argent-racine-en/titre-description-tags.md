## ➤ Video description: 

"Money is the root of all evil": the Bible does not say it that way. 1 Timothy 6:10 (World English Bible): "For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil." It is not "money," it is "the love of money," and it is "a" root, not "the" root.

The verse goes on about those who were led astray from the faith in their greed. Luke 12:15 and Hebrews 13:5 point the same way: "Keep yourselves from covetousness" and "Be free from the love of money, content with such things as you have." These texts do not condemn money in itself: they warn against attachment to it. The real question is not "am I allowed to buy?" but "what owns me?"
