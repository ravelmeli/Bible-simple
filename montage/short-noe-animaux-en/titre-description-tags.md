## ➤ Video description : 

Did Noah bring two of every animal onto the ark? Genesis says more: in chapter 6, two of every kind, a male and a female; in chapter 7, seven pairs of clean animals and just one pair of the others. Find out in under a minute what the text really says.

The text doesn't say why there are more clean animals, but after the flood Noah offers sacrifices of clean animals (Genesis 8). Some scholars see in these two instructions two traditions woven together: that is a hypothesis, not a certainty. And the Hebrew word min, translated "species," means "kind," not our modern idea of a species.

📖 KEY VERSE: "You shall take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female; and of the animals that are not clean two, the male and his female." — Genesis 7:2 (WEB)
