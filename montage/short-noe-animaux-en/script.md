True or false: Noah brought two of every animal onto the ark?
That's what almost everyone believes. Yet the text says something else.
Yes, Genesis chapter six speaks of two of every kind, a male and a female.
But in chapter seven, God gives more detail. Here is the verse, in the World English Bible: "You shall take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female; and of the animals that are not clean two, the male and his female."
Seven pairs of the clean animals. Just one pair of the others.
The clean ones are those the law allows people to eat or to offer in sacrifice.
Why take more of them? The text doesn't say. But after the flood, Noah offers sacrifices on an altar, using clean animals.
One more detail: the Hebrew word translated "species" is min, which means "kind." It is not our modern idea of a species.
Some scholars see in these two instructions two traditions woven together. Others read a clarification. That is a hypothesis, not a certainty.
So, true or false? Neither: the picture is incomplete.
