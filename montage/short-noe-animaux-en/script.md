True or false: Noah brought two of every animal onto the ark?
Genesis actually says something else.
In chapter seven, God gives more detail, in the World English Bible: "You shall take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female; and of the animals that are not clean two, the male and his female."
Seven pairs of clean animals, the ones that can be offered in sacrifice. Just one pair of the others.
The text doesn't say why. After the flood, though, Noah offers sacrifices on an altar.
Chapter six, for its part, speaks of two of every kind.
Some see two traditions woven together: that is a hypothesis, not a certainty.
And the Hebrew word translated "species," min, means "kind," not our modern species.
So, true or false? Neither: it's incomplete.
