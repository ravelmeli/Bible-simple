## ➤ Video description: 

"This too shall pass": almost everyone thinks that sentence is in the Bible. It does not appear in any book of the Bible. It comes from a legend about King Solomon found in Jewish and Persian traditions, and it spread in English in the nineteenth century, notably in a speech by Abraham Lincoln in 1859.

What the Bible says is different. Ecclesiastes 3: "For everything there is a season, and a time for every purpose under heaven." Psalm 30: "Weeping may stay for the night, but joy comes in the morning." The text does not promise that everything passes quickly: it says that pain has its time, and that it does not have the last word. Only the word of God stands forever (Isaiah 40). If your pain lasts, talk to a loved one or to a professional: it is not a lack of faith.
