## Voice
Voice generated by the channel owner (Gemini TTS, mp3 converted), 11 min 31 originally, 11 min 20 after shortening 31 pauses over 0.6 s to 0.4 s; normalised to about −19 LUFS. **The voice adds one sentence that is not in the French version**: after "it describes an immense wealth", it says "Now, the Queen of Sheba had just said that the Lord wanted Solomon on his throne to rule with justice and right" (this matches 1 Kings 10:9 in substance). The script and the montage follow the voice. The Hebrew letter names are read in English ("noon, kof, samek"). Uncut file in `assets/source/voix-originale.wav`.
