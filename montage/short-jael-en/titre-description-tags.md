## ➤ Description de la video : 

One woman with no army ended twenty years of war. Her name is Jael. In one minute, discover the story of Judges 4 and 5, and what the text says (and doesn't say) about her act.

The king of Canaan has oppressed Israel for twenty years. His commander, Sisera, leads nine hundred iron chariots. The prophetess Deborah tells the Israelite general Barak: "The journey that you take won't be for your honor; for Yahweh will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman." Sisera, defeated, flees on foot to Jael's tent, where she welcomes him, gives him milk, then kills him in his sleep with a tent peg and a hammer (Judges 4:17-21). The text doesn't say why Jael chose this side. Deborah's song celebrates her: "Most blessed of women be Jael" (Judges 5:24). The surprising part: the glory didn't go to the general, but to a woman with no sword.
