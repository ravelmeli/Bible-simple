How many animals did

Noah bring onto the

ark?

Two of every kind?

Not quite.

In Genesis chapter 6,

God does say:

two of every kind,

a male and a

female.

But in chapter 7,

he adds:

“You shall take seven

pairs of every clean

animal with you,

the male and his

female.

Of the animals that

are not clean,

take two.” Why more

clean animals?

The story gives a

clue:

when Noah leaves the

ark,

he offers sacrifices of

clean animals.

With only one pair,

the species would have

died out.

Here's the surprising part:

the law of Moses

doesn't exist yet,

and yet the difference

between clean and unclean

is already there.

In Hebrew,

the text says “seven, seven.”

Seven pairs or seven

animals?

Translators disagree.

Some scholars see two

stories woven together; others

a rule followed by

a detail.

Did you know that?

Tell me in the

comments.
