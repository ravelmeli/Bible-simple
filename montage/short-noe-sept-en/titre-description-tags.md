## ➤ Description de la video : 

How many animals did Noah bring onto the ark? Two of every kind? Not quite. In one minute, discover the detail in Genesis 7 that almost everyone forgets.

In Genesis chapter 6, God does ask for two of every kind, a male and a female. But in chapter 7, he adds: seven pairs of every clean animal, and only one pair of the unclean ones. The story itself gives a clue: when Noah leaves the ark, he offers sacrifices of clean animals (Genesis 8:20); with only one pair, the species would have died out. The surprising part: the Law of Moses on clean and unclean (Leviticus 11) doesn't exist yet, and yet the distinction is already there. In Hebrew, the text says "seven, seven": seven pairs or seven animals, translators disagree. Some scholars see two stories woven together; others read a general rule followed by a detail.

📖 KEY VERSE: "You shall take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female. Of the animals that are not clean, take two, the male and his female." — Genesis 7:2 (WEB)
