## ➤ Description de la video : 

You might say it every week without knowing what it means: Amen. In a minute and a half, discover the meaning of this small Hebrew word, far stronger than a simple "so be it."

In Hebrew, amen comes from a root that means "solid," "firm," "reliable"; the same root also gives the words "faith" and "truth." In Exodus, when Aaron and Hur hold up Moses' arms, the text says his hands were "steady": the same root. Isaiah plays on it: "If you will not believe, surely you shall not be established." When Ezra reads the Law, all the people answer "Amen! Amen!" Saying amen means: this is solid, I'm leaning on it. The surprising part: Jesus puts amen at the beginning of his sentences, which our Bibles translate as "Truly, I tell you." And in Revelation, Jesus himself is called "the Amen." The word passed unchanged into Greek, Latin, Arabic and many other languages.
