## ➤ Video description : 

Is "hallelujah" only a cry of joy? In Hebrew, it is a command: Hallelu-Yah, "Praise Yah!" Find out in one minute where this word comes from, what it means and how it reached us.

Hallelu comes from the verb halal, "to praise," in the plural imperative: the call is addressed to a whole group. Yah is a short form of God's name, YHWH. You find this word mostly in the Psalms, the last of which ends with Hallelu-Yah. Greek wrote it without translating it, then Latin, then our languages. In the New Testament, it appears only in Revelation (chapter 19). This isn't against joy: praising is often singing. But the word itself is first of all a call to praise together.
