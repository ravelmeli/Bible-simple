## ➤ Video description : 

The Bible is not a single book: it is a library, written over about fifteen centuries according to tradition, from the time of Moses (around 1400 BC) to Revelation (end of the first century). Many scholars suggest a little over a thousand years instead: the exact span is debated.

The word "Bible" comes from the Greek biblia, "the books". Most Protestant Bibles count 66 books. About forty authors worked on it (a king like David, a shepherd like Amos, a physician like Luke), in three languages: Hebrew, a little Aramaic and Greek. For believers, one thread runs through these texts: the relationship between God and humans. Each text reads better in its own time.
