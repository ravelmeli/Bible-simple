Did you know the

Bible was written over

about 1,500 years?

It's not a single

book,

it's a library.

The word "Bible" comes

from the Greek biblia,

which means "the books."

Most Protestant Bibles count

sixty-six.

The oldest texts go

back,

according to tradition,

to the time of

Moses,

around 1400 BC.

The last one,

Revelation,

dates from the end

of the first century.

That makes about fifteen

centuries.

Many scholars suggest a

little over a thousand

years instead.

The exact span is

debated.

About forty authors worked

on it:

a king like David,

a shepherd like Amos,

a physician like Luke.

Three languages:

Hebrew,

a little Aramaic,

and Greek.

For believers,

one thread runs through

all these texts:

the relationship between God

and humans.

So reading the Bible

means crossing centuries:

each text reads better

in its own time.

Bible do you want

to read first?
