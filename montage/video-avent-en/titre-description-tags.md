## ➤ Video description : 

The word "Advent" appears nowhere in the Bible, and yet the whole text speaks of waiting. Advent 2026 begins on Sunday, November 29: where do the wreath, the calendar, the four weeks and the date of December 25 come from? In this study, we separate what the Bible says, what tradition says and what is a modern habit.

You will see the meaning of the words adventus (Latin), parousia and epiphaneia (Greek), biblical waiting (Isaiah 7, 9, 11 and 40, Micah 5, Jeremiah 33), the debate on almah and parthenos, the people of Luke (Zechariah, Mary, Joseph, Simeon and Anna), the Aramaic word maranatha, the origin of the wreath (19th century, Germany) and the Hebrew word tiqvah, hope. Hypotheses are presented as such, especially for the date of December 25.

If this season is hard for you (loneliness, grief, exhaustion), talking to a loved one, a pastor or a health professional can help.
