The Gospels are written

in Greek,

yet Jesus spoke Aramaic.

In first-century Palestine,

it was the language

of everyday life.

The Gospel writers wrote

in Greek,

but they kept a

few words,

just as he said

them.

Before a little girl

To a deaf man,

he says,

Ephphatha,

that is,

be opened.

In prayer at Gethsemane,

he calls God Abba,

that is,

Father.

And on the cross,

he cries Eloi,

Eloi,

lama sabachthani,

which means,

Did he also know

Hebrew and a little

Greek?

Many historians think so,

but it's a hypothesis.

The text doesn't say.

Here's the surprising part.

His most intimate words

at a child's bedside,

in prayer,

on the cross,

stayed in his mother

tongue.

you most?
