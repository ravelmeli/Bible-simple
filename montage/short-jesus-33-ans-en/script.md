"Jesus died at 33." The Bible never says that.
No verse gives his age at death.
The only number is Luke 3, verse 23, in the World English Bible: "Jesus himself, when he began to teach, was about thirty years old."
Notice the word "about." Luke does not give an exact age.
Then you have to count. John mentions at least three Passover feasts during his ministry, which makes two to three years.
As for his birth, Matthew places it before the death of Herod the Great, in 4 BC.
With these landmarks, many scholars place his death between AD 30 and 33. He would then be about 33 to 37 years old. It is not a certainty.
So "33" is an old tradition, not a number from the text.
The Gospels are not interested in his age, but in what he said and did.
