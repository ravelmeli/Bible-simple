## ➤ Video description : 

"Jesus died at 33": the Bible never says that. No verse gives his age at death. The only landmark is Luke 3:23: "Jesus himself, when he began to teach, was about thirty years old", an approximate age, at the start of his ministry.

To estimate his age at death, you have to count: John mentions at least three Passover feasts during his ministry (two to three years), and Matthew places his birth before the death of Herod the Great, in 4 BC. Many scholars place his death between AD 30 and 33, which would make him about 33 to 37: an estimate, not a certainty. "33" is an old tradition, not a number from the text. The Gospels are less interested in his age than in what he said and did.
